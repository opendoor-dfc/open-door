## The challenge

Many girls lack specialised support for their professional growth, and rigid, one-size-fits-all systems make it harder. Without targeted resources or mentorship, it is difficult to keep developing skills and build a career.

## How OPEN DOOR helps

- **Capacity building:** courses and creative learning formats such as storytelling, illustration, mind mapping, eco-challenges, comics and elevator pitches.
- **The Global Podcast:** conversations with experts from around the world. Planned.
- **Community exchange clubs:** members share opportunities with a short description and the official link.
- **Live workshops:** hands-on sessions in high schools and universities.

All content is either created by our team or submitted by Ambassadors and checked by us.

## Our vision

We want to build a leadership pipeline. Ambassadors can grow step by step:

1. **Ambassador and grassroots advocate**
2. **Country or Regional Coordinator**
3. **Advisor** shaping our global strategy

Experienced members mentor newcomers, so the community keeps growing. Every young woman should be able to access opportunities for self-development from home, whatever her location, financial means or safety concerns.

OPEN DOOR contributes to the UN Sustainable Development Goals 4 (Quality Education) and 5 (Gender Equality).
